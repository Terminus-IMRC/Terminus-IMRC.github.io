Yukimasa Sugizaki (杉崎 行優; 杉﨑 行優), Ph.D.

Research

My research focuses on the design and implementation of high-performance arithmetic and cryptographic algorithms for modern computing architectures. In particular, I work on:

  • Architecture-specific optimization for CPUs and GPUs, including Intel AVX-512, Arm SVE, and NVIDIA Tensor Cores
  • High-performance modular arithmetic, including Barrett, Shoup, and Montgomery algorithms
  • Number theoretic transform (NTT) for polynomial and cryptographic computation
  • Efficient implementation of cryptographic schemes, including homomorphic encryption
